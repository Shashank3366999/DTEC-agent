# Collect directions inside Dtec (for the person on site)

Nothing about the inside of Dtec is published, so real directions are our advantage. Walk each route **starting from where the robot will stand**. Send photos and a voice note for each one to the team chat. The remote team adds them to the agent.

## How to record each place

For each place, send:
1. **A voice note** describing the walk in simple steps with landmarks, like a visitor would hear it.
   Example: "From the robot, walk past the reception desk. The lifts are on your left. Take the lift to the first floor, turn right, it's the second door."
2. **Walking time** from the robot, in minutes (use your phone's timer).
3. **One or two photos** of the key landmark (the lifts, the sign, the door).
4. Anything visitors need to know: needs an access card, closed after a certain time, and so on.
