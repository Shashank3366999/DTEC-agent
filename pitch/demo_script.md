# Demo script: three routes, 3 minutes in total

Each route is one take. Cuts only between routes. Visitor, robot **and operator** in the shot, `/live` visible if possible. Two languages in every route. Restart the bridge at the start spot before each route.

## Route 1: the easy route (about 50 s)

| Who | What |
|---|---|
| Visitor (Arabic) | «أبي مكان هادي أسوي فيه مكالمة.» ("I want a quiet place to take a call.") |
| Robot | Asks in Arabic if a call booth will do |
| Visitor | «إي، الثانية.» ("Yes, the second one.") |
| Robot | Arabic to the visitor, then English: "Operator: ..." |
| Operator | Walks. "Okay, that step is done." after each step |
| Robot | "Operator: stop here, please. We have arrived." Then Arabic: we've arrived |

## Route 2: background music (about 50 s, their music)

Arabic visitor: «جوعان، وين الأكل؟» ("I'm hungry, where is the food?") → offers the kitchen → guides → arrives. Stand close, about 1 metre. Optional: the visitor switches to English on the way.

## Route 3: change of destination (about 80 s, they choose it)

Start with an Arabic visitor and any place. Halfway, the second person asks for the place the organisers named, in English (or Hindi for a third language). The robot stops the operator, tells **both** people, plans from where it stands, arrives, and offers the first person to go on.

**Practise** with a teammate as the operator, following word for word even when it is wrong. Time it: over 3 minutes, shorten the walks, not the talking.
