# Demo script (max 2 minutes, one unedited take)

**Setup:** the camera sees the robot **and** the reception screen next to it. The screen is reset (empty). One person plays the visitor and speaks Arabic. Someone holds a phone ready to scan the QR code.

| # | Visitor says / does | Robot does | Screen | Shows the jury |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Says the wake word, then «مرحبا» ("Hello") | Greets in Arabic, says it's an AI guide | empty | Says it's an AI |
| 2 | «عندي اجتماع مع أوراسيا كابيتال الساعة ثلاثة، وأبي أصلي قبل.» ("I have a meeting at Oraseya Capital at three, and I want to pray first.") | Short answer: DSO Masjid in the Techno Point building first, then Oraseya Capital on the first floor of TechnoHub 1. Offers to tell Oraseya she's here | | Arabic, two goals in one question, short answers |
| 3 | «إيه، اسمي سارة» ("Yes, my name is Sara") | "Done, Oraseya Capital knows you're here." | **New arrival: Sara · Oraseya Capital · 15:00** | An action going through |
| 4 | Switches to English: "Wait, where's the bank?" | Asks which bank, or suggests Emirates NBD in Silicon Central Mall | | Clarifying question + language switch |
| 5 | "Can you send me the way to the mosque?" | Sends the route and asks her to scan the QR code | **Route card + QR code** | Second action |
| 6 | Scans the QR code with the phone on camera | | Phone shows the route card | Route on her phone |
| 7 | "What's the Wi-Fi password at Oraseya?" | "I don't know that, sorry. The reception desk can help." | | Honest, no made-up answers |
| 8 | "Thanks!" | Wishes her a good meeting | | Natural ending |

**Timing:** practise and time it. If it runs over 2 minutes, cut step 4 first, then step 7.

**Remember:** keep the camera on both the robot and the screen, speak clearly about 1 metre from the robot, and wait for the robot to finish before talking (unless you are showing an interruption).
