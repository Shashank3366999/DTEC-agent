## Where the robot is

The robot stands in the ground-floor lobby of Dtec TechnoHub 1, Dubai Silicon Oasis, a few steps inside the main glass entrance doors. All directions start from the robot. [Demo data]

## Dtec

- Dtec (Dubai Technology Entrepreneur Campus) has two campuses: TechnoHub 1, and building A5 in Dubai Digital Park. [DSO]
- Dtec's main campus is in TechnoHub 1 and 2, Dubai Silicon Oasis. [Public]
- Dtec office hours are 07:30 to 15:00. Members have 24/7 access. [Dtec]
- Dtec website: dtec dot ae. DSO toll-free number: 600 59 55 57, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 15:00. [DSO]

## Companies

## Around Dubai Silicon Oasis

### Shopping and food
- Silicon Central Mall: open 08:00 to 00:00 (midnight). [Listing]
  - Friends Avenue Cafe is on the ground floor. [Listing]
  - Emirates NBD (bank) is on the first floor. [Listing]
  - Lulu Hypermarket and a cinema are in the mall, and it has underground parking. [Public]
- Open 24 hours: Union Coop Silicon Oasis, Souq Extra, and Life Pharmacy in Palace Towers. [Listing]

### Prayer
- DSO Masjid: in the Techno Point building, with free parking. [Listing] The Techno Point building is also where RIT Dubai university is. [Public]
- Al Waha Masjid: open 04:00 to 23:00. [Listing]
- Sheikha Ali Mosque: in DSO next to Dubai Digital Park, room for about 650 people. [Public]
- Closest place to pray without leaving Dtec: the prayer room inside TechnoHub 1 (see the table above).

### Health
- Burjeel Medical Center DSO: open daily 07:00 to 23:00. [Listing]
- Life Medical Centre: open daily 07:00 to 23:00. [Listing]
- Emergency: call 999.

### Getting around
- Bus routes 50, 320, 365, 366 and X25 serve Dubai Silicon Oasis. [Research]
- To DSO Masjid in Techno Point, or to Silicon Central Mall: a short taxi ride from the Dtec taxi pick-up. [Demo data] Exact walking times between buildings are not known, so suggest a taxi or a maps app and offer to send the route to the visitor's phone.

## Doers Summit Dubai 2026

- The Doers Summit is a tech and startup summit hosted by Dubai Silicon Oasis this week, with more than 100 speakers from companies such as Meta, Intel, Nvidia, IBM, Salesforce, Spotify and Bosch. [Public]
- The full agenda is on the summit website, doerssummit dot com. The guide does not have the session times, so for times and rooms send visitors to the summit website or the summit staff. [Public]
- The Voice of the Oasis hackathon winners present on the Doers Summit main stage on Wednesday at 13:00. [Organisers]
